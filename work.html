01

Content marketing

×

Impact Negotiation Group helps enterprise commercial teams in high-stakes negotiations through practical training. I developed and tested educational campaigns to help potential buyers explore their negotiation needs and take a next step.

144MQLs in three months
€150K+tracked pipeline over four months
  • 30+ LinkedIn ads and four lead generation campaigns.
  • Two landing pages and three downloadable resources sharing niche frameworks.
  • A negotiation quiz integrated into the website.
  • 20+ thought leadership posts, including a targeted video series.
  • An email nurture sequence for MQLs.

What worked

  • The two playbook campaigns generated 74 MQLs in three months.
  • A checklist generated 49 MQLs in two months.
  • The quiz generated 21 MQLs.

What didn’t work

The free consultation generated 3 SQLs, but its high cost and low conversion made it uneconomical to scale.

Demand Generation & ABM

×

SurePay helps banks prevent fraud and misdirected payments; 9 in 10 Dutch banks already use its solution. The goal was to build demand among European banks through focused account targeting and relevant content.

  • Defined the ICP for medium to large banks and mapped buyers across payments, compliance, fraud, IT, security and privacy.
  • Built and enriched a tiered list of target banks, checking accounts against the ICP.
  • Produced SEO articles, expert-led LinkedIn posts, educational videos, carousels and single-image ads.
  • Connected the content to blogs, white papers and demo booking pages; promoted it through LinkedIn Ads and retargeted engaged audiences.
